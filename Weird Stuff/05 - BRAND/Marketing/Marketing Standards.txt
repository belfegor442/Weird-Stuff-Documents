MARKETING STANDARDS

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: Weird Stuff
Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

These standards govern public promotional communication by Weird Stuff.

[MANDATORY]
Marketing must be accurate and must not knowingly misrepresent product capabilities, availability, security, pricing, status, or ownership.

[MANDATORY]
Experimental, beta, preview, and development products must be identified accurately where their status is material.

[MANDATORY]
Third-party trademarks and assets must be used according to applicable rights and licenses.

2. CLAIMS

[MANDATORY]
Performance or security claims must have a reasonable factual basis.

[MANDATORY]
Testimonials, comparisons, and endorsements must not be fabricated.

3. PUBLICATION

[RECOMMENDED]
Material announcements should be reviewed before publication.

[PENDING]
Formal campaign approval workflow.
[PENDING]
Formal advertising archive.
