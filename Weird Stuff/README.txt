WEIRD STUFF
DOCUMENTATION ROOT

Version: 1.0
Created: September 7, 2026

PURPOSE
This directory is the master working documentation structure for
Weird Stuff.

FOLDER STRUCTURE

01 - CORPORATE
Company organization, governance, document control.

02 - LEGAL
Terms, privacy, cookies, acceptable use, security, intellectual
property, and custom application policies.

03 - PRODUCTS
Product-specific documentation.

04 - OPERATIONS
Development, QA, design, infrastructure, and security procedures.

05 - BRAND
Brand identity and marketing standards.

06 - PUBLIC
Approved public-facing communications.

07 - INTERNAL
Internal team and communication standards.

08 - ARCHIVE
Superseded or retired documentation.

IMPORTANT
Some legal identity and infrastructure details are intentionally
not included because they are restricted information.

This is not a claim that the legal documents are independently
binding against third parties. Public legal documents must be
reviewed against the actual legal structure and applicable law
before publication.

DOCUMENT RULE
Do not delete superseded documents from the archive.
Do not publish internal documents as public legal terms.
Do not publish restricted information.
