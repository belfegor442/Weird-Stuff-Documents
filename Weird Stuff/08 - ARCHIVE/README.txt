WEIRD STUFF
ARCHIVE

Version: 1.1
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

The archive keeps superseded, retired, and historical documentation that still needs to be preserved for reference and traceability.

[MANDATORY]
Archived documents must not be treated as current policy, current procedure, or current product specifications.

[MANDATORY]
Documents must not be deleted solely because they have been superseded when historical retention is required.

2. RETIRED PRODUCTS

Documentation for retired products may remain in the archive after the product has been withdrawn from active development or publication.

3. LEGACY MATERIAL

Legacy material may be incomplete or no longer reflect current practices. Its historical status must remain clear so that it is not mistaken for current documentation.

END OF DOCUMENT
