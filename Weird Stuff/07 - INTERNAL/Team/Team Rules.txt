WEIRD STUFF
TEAM RULES

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: INTERNAL

[MANDATORY]
Team members must operate within their assigned authority and responsibilities.

[MANDATORY]
Members must protect internal, restricted, and confidential information.

[MANDATORY]
Members must not intentionally interfere with another member's authorized work, accounts, repositories, or infrastructure.

[MANDATORY]
Security incidents and significant operational problems must be escalated through the appropriate channel.

[MANDATORY]
When a member leaves a role or Weird Stuff, applicable access must be revoked.

[RECOMMENDED]
Members should document important work so that responsibilities can be transferred when necessary.
