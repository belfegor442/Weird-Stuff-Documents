WEIRD STUFF
QA STANDARDS

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: Weird Stuff
Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

QA verifies that releases meet their intended functional, security, stability, and compatibility requirements.

2. TESTING

[MANDATORY]
Stable releases must undergo testing appropriate to their functionality and risk.

[MANDATORY]
Known critical defects must be evaluated before stable release.

[RECOMMENDED]
Major changes should receive regression testing.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important fixes should retain evidence that the fix was tested.

3. BUG REPORTING

[MANDATORY]
Important bugs must contain enough information to determine impact and, where possible, reproduce the issue.

A useful bug report should identify:

* Affected product/version.
* Expected behavior.
* Actual behavior.
* Reproduction steps.
* Relevant logs or evidence.
* Severity or impact.

4. RELEASE GATES

[MANDATORY]
A release must not be marked stable solely because development is complete.

[MANDATORY]
Known unresolved critical issues must be explicitly accepted by the responsible authority or block release according to project risk.

[PENDING]
Formal severity matrix.
[PENDING]
Formal test-case management system.

END OF DOCUMENT
