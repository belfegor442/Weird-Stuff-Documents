WEIRD STUFF
ACCESS CONTROL

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: Weird Stuff
Classification: RESTRICTED

1. PRINCIPLES

[MANDATORY]
Access must be based on operational need.

[MANDATORY]
Permissions must be limited to what is reasonably necessary.

[MANDATORY]
Credentials must not be shared.

[MANDATORY]
Access must be revoked when the operational need ends.

2. ROLE-BASED ACCESS

Access should correspond to the person's actual role and assigned responsibilities.

[MANDATORY]
A role change requires an access review.

[MANDATORY]
Termination or removal requires prompt revocation of applicable access.

3. ADMINISTRATIVE ACCESS

[MANDATORY]
Administrative access must receive stronger protection than ordinary access.

[RECOMMENDED]
Multi-factor authentication should be enabled on critical accounts where available.

[RECOMMENDED]
Critical permissions should be reviewed periodically.

4. CREDENTIAL INCIDENTS

[MANDATORY]
Suspected credential compromise must be reported and the affected credential revoked, rotated, or otherwise secured as soon as reasonably possible.

[PENDING]
Formal access register.
[PENDING]
Formal approval workflow.
