WEIRD STUFF
DESIGN STANDARDS

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: Weird Stuff
Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

These standards define the baseline requirements for product and communication design.

2. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

[MANDATORY]
Design must serve a defined product, communication, or usability objective.

[MANDATORY]
Design assets must not intentionally misrepresent product capabilities or status.

[MANDATORY]
Brand assets must follow the approved Weird Stuff brand guidelines.

[RECOMMENDED]
Interfaces should prioritize readability, consistency, accessibility, and predictable interaction.

3. PRODUCT UI

[MANDATORY]
Critical actions must have distinguishable states and appropriate feedback.

[MANDATORY]
Error states must communicate enough information for the user to understand the problem without exposing secrets.

[RECOMMENDED]
Repeated interface patterns should behave consistently across a product.

4. ASSETS

[MANDATORY]
Third-party assets must be used according to their applicable licenses.

[MANDATORY]
Final assets must be stored in an authorized repository or asset system.

[PENDING]
Formal asset naming convention.
[PENDING]
Formal design review workflow.

END OF DOCUMENT
