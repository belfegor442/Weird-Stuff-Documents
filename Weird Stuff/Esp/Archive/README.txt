ARCHIVE

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

The archive preserves superseded, retired, and historical documentation for traceability.

[MANDATORY]
Archived documents must not be treated as current policy or current product specifications.

[MANDATORY]
Documents must not be deleted solely because they are superseded when historical retention is required.

2. RETIRED PRODUCTS

Retired product documentation may remain here after withdrawal from active development or publication.

3. LEGACY MATERIAL

Legacy material may be incomplete. Its historical status must remain clear.

END OF DOCUMENT
