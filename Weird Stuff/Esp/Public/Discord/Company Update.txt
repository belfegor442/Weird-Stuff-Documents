WEIRD STUFF
PUBLIC DISCORD COMPANY UPDATE STANDARD

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: PUBLIC

1. PURPOSE

This document defines the baseline for official Weird Stuff company updates distributed through Discord.

2. REQUIREMENTS

[MANDATORY]
Official updates must be truthful and attributable to Weird Stuff.

[MANDATORY]
Confidential or restricted internal information must not be included.

[MANDATORY]
Product withdrawals, retirements, major releases, and significant organizational changes must be described accurately.

[RECOMMENDED]
Updates should state the affected product or area, current status, effective date where relevant, and the practical consequence for users or team members.

3. SECURITY

[MANDATORY]
Credentials, tokens, private infrastructure information, and other secrets must never be published.

END OF DOCUMENT
