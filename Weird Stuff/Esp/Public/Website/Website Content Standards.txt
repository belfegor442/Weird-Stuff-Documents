WEIRD STUFF
WEBSITE CONTENT STANDARDS

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: PUBLIC

1. PURPOSE

These standards govern official Weird Stuff website content.

[MANDATORY]
Published information must accurately represent Weird Stuff, its products, and their status.

[MANDATORY]
Public legal documents must not contain unresolved placeholders when presented as effective legal documents.

[MANDATORY]
Internal, restricted, confidential, and reserved information must not be published.

2. PRODUCT STATUS

Product pages must distinguish active, development, beta, experimental, suspended, and retired products when applicable.

3. LEGAL CONTENT

[MANDATORY]
Terms, privacy, cookie, acceptable-use, intellectual-property, security, and custom-application documents must be consistent with their approved versions.

4. ACCESSIBILITY AND QUALITY

[RECOMMENDED]
Pages should be readable, navigable, responsive, and accessible to users with different devices and abilities.

[PENDING]
Formal accessibility baseline.
[PENDING]
Formal content review schedule.

END OF DOCUMENT
