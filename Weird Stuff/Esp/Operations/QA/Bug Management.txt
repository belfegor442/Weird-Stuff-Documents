1. PURPOSE

This document defines the baseline lifecycle for defects and operational issues.

2. LIFECYCLE

REPORTED -> TRIAGED -> ASSIGNED -> INVESTIGATING -> FIXED -> VERIFIED -> CLOSED

An issue may instead be rejected, duplicated, deferred, or reopened when appropriate.

3. REPORTING

[MANDATORY]
Important defects must be recorded in an authorized tracking system.

[MANDATORY]
Reports must contain enough information to assess impact and reproduce the issue where possible.

4. TRIAGE

Issues should be evaluated according to:

* Security impact.
* Data integrity impact.
* Availability impact.
* User impact.
* Reproducibility.
* Scope.

5. SEVERITY

CRITICAL
Immediate or potentially severe impact to security, data integrity, or essential operation.

HIGH
Major functional, security, or reliability impact.

MEDIUM
Meaningful defect with limited scope or workaround.

LOW
Minor defect with limited operational impact.

INFORMATIONAL
Observation or improvement without material defect impact.

6. CLOSURE

[MANDATORY]
A defect marked fixed must be verified before closure when verification is technically possible.

[MANDATORY]
Reopened issues must retain their prior history.

END OF DOCUMENT
