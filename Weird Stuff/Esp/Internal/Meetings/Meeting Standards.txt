WEIRD STUFF
MEETING STANDARDS

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE

Meetings should exist to make decisions, coordinate work, resolve issues, or transfer relevant information.

2. REQUIREMENTS

[RECOMMENDED]
Meetings should have a defined purpose and relevant participants.

[RECOMMENDED]
Important decisions should be recorded with the decision, responsible person, and relevant follow-up.

[MANDATORY]
Restricted information must only be discussed with authorized participants.

[MANDATORY]
Meeting records must be stored according to their information classification.

[PENDING]
Formal meeting-record template.
[PENDING]
Formal retention period.

END OF DOCUMENT
