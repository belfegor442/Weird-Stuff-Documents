1. PURPOSE

This document defines the intended role, architecture, security,
privacy, behavior, and limitations of artificial intelligence
functionality within Monix.

AI functionality may evolve during development and must not be
considered final until explicitly defined and released.

5. LOCAL AI
===========

If AI processing is performed locally:

[MANDATORY]

Information processed locally must remain on the device unless
another feature explicitly transmits it externally.

[MANDATORY]

Local AI functionality must respect the same security boundaries
as other Monix subsystems.

============================================================
6. REMOTE AI
============

If an external AI service is used:

[MANDATORY]

Data must not be transmitted to the external provider without an
appropriate purpose and authorization.

[MANDATORY]

Sensitive information must not be transmitted unnecessarily.

[MANDATORY]

External AI processing must comply with the applicable privacy
requirements.

7. AI INPUTS
============

Possible AI inputs may include:

* User-provided questions.
* Selected logs.
* Selected monitoring information.
* Diagnostic information.
* Configuration information.
* Other approved data.

[MANDATORY]

AI inputs must be limited to the scope required for the requested
operation.

============================================================
8. SENSITIVE INFORMATION
========================

Potentially sensitive information may include:

* Passwords.
* API keys.
* Authentication tokens.
* Private keys.
* Personal data.
* Internal system paths.
* Confidential logs.
* Security-sensitive information.

[MANDATORY]

Secrets must not intentionally be provided to an AI subsystem
unless strictly required, authorized, and appropriately protected.

[MANDATORY]

AI functionality must not be used as a justification for
unnecessary collection or disclosure of sensitive information.

============================================================
9. AI OUTPUT
============

AI output may include:

* Explanations.
* Recommendations.
* Summaries.
* Diagnostic hypotheses.
* Suggested actions.
* Natural-language interpretations.

[MANDATORY]

AI-generated information must not automatically be treated as
verified system information.

[MANDATORY]

AI output must not silently override authoritative system
measurements or deterministic security results.

============================================================
10. HALLUCINATIONS AND INCORRECT OUTPUT
=======================================

AI systems may produce inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading
information.

[MANDATORY]

Monix must not present uncertain AI output as a guaranteed fact.

[RECOMMENDED]

Important recommendations should be accompanied by enough context
for the User to verify the underlying information.

============================================================
11. SECURITY DECISIONS
======================

AI may assist with security analysis.

[MANDATORY]

AI output must not independently authorize high-impact security
operations.

[MANDATORY]

Critical actions such as process termination, isolation, SCRAM
activation, or other high-impact operations must be controlled by
deterministic authorization and validation mechanisms.

============================================================
12. AI AND MONITORING
=====================

AI may interpret monitoring information.

Example:

MONITORING
->
CPU / RAM / GPU / TEMP / PROCESS DATA
->
AI ANALYSIS
->
EXPLANATION

[MANDATORY]

AI interpretation must remain distinguishable from the underlying
measured telemetry.

============================================================
13. AI AND LOGGING
==================

AI may analyze selected logs.

[MANDATORY]

Only logs necessary for the requested analysis should be supplied.

[MANDATORY]

Secrets must be filtered or protected before transmission to an
external provider.

[RECOMMENDED]

Sensitive values should be redacted before AI processing.

============================================================
14. AI AND CONFIGURATION
========================

AI may suggest configuration changes.

[MANDATORY]

AI-generated configuration recommendations must not automatically
be applied to security-critical settings.

[MANDATORY]

Configuration changes must still pass the normal validation
pipeline.

============================================================
15. AI AND SNAPSHOTS
====================

AI may analyze selected snapshot data.

[MANDATORY]

Only relevant snapshot information should be provided.

[MANDATORY]

Snapshot contents must continue to follow the applicable privacy
and security requirements during AI processing.

============================================================
16. AI ACTIONS
==============

AI functionality may suggest actions such as:

* Restarting a feature.
* Changing a configuration.
* Investigating an event.
* Running a diagnostic.
* Adjusting a non-critical setting.

[MANDATORY]

AI-generated suggestions must not automatically be treated as
authorized actions.

[MANDATORY]

High-impact operations require independent validation and
authorization.

20. AI LOGGING
==============

AI operations may generate internal logs.

Possible information includes:

* Request identifier.
* Feature used.
* Processing result.
* Error state.
* Provider failure.

[MANDATORY]

[RECOMMENDED]

============================================================
21. AI FAILURE MODES
====================

AI functionality may fail because of:

* Provider outage.
* Model error.
* Invalid input.
* Network failure.
* Resource exhaustion.
* Local model failure.
* Configuration failure.

[MANDATORY]

AI failure must not prevent unrelated Monix functionality from
operating unless the AI functionality is required for that
function.

23. EXTERNAL PROVIDER SECURITY
==============================

If an external AI provider is used:

[MANDATORY]

The provider must be evaluated according to the applicable
security and privacy requirements.

Relevant considerations may include:

* Data handling.
* Encryption.
* Authentication.
* Retention.
* Sub-processors.
* International transfers.
* Security incidents.

============================================================
24. PROMPT INJECTION
====================

AI functionality may process text originating from Users, logs,
files, or other sources.

[MANDATORY]

Untrusted text must not automatically be treated as trusted
instructions for privileged Monix operations.

[MANDATORY]

AI-generated instructions must not bypass normal authorization
controls.

============================================================
25. TOOL USE
============

If the AI subsystem is capable of invoking tools or actions:

[MANDATORY]

Tool access must be explicitly defined.

[MANDATORY]

Each tool must have an appropriate authorization boundary.

[MANDATORY]

The AI must not gain privileges merely because it generated a
request to use a tool.

26. AI OUTPUT VALIDATION
========================

Where AI output can influence product behavior:

AI OUTPUT
->
VALIDATION
->
POLICY CHECK
->
AUTHORIZATION
->
EXECUTION

[MANDATORY]

Validation must occur before AI output is converted into an
action affecting protected resources.

============================================================
27. AI PERFORMANCE
==================

AI processing may consume:

* CPU.
* RAM.
* GPU.
* Network bandwidth.
* Storage.

[MANDATORY]

AI functionality must not intentionally create uncontrolled
resource consumption.

[RECOMMENDED]

Expensive AI processing should be isolated from critical UI
operations where practical.

============================================================
28. AI AVAILABILITY
===================

AI functionality may depend on external or local resources.

[MANDATORY]

Failure of AI services must be handled without falsely reporting
successful AI processing.

============================================================
29. AI TESTING
==============

AI functionality should be tested for:

* Incorrect output.
* Missing context.
* Invalid input.
* Sensitive data leakage.
* Prompt injection.
* Tool misuse.
* Unauthorized actions.
* Provider failures.
* Network failures.
* Resource exhaustion.
* Recovery behavior.

[MANDATORY]

AI must not be capable of bypassing mandatory security controls
through normal user-facing functionality.

============================================================
30. AI LIMITATIONS
==================

The following limitations may apply:

* AI output may be inaccurate.
* AI output may be incomplete.
* AI may misunderstand context.
* AI may provide an incorrect diagnosis.
* AI may depend on incomplete telemetry.

[MANDATORY]

These limitations must not be concealed when they are relevant
to the reliability of the result.

============================================================
31. PUBLIC CLAIMS
=================

[MANDATORY]

Weird Stuff must not make claims that Monix's AI can guarantee
correct diagnosis, complete security detection, or infallible
decision-making unless such claims are demonstrably supported.
