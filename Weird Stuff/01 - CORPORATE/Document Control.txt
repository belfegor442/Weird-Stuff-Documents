WEIRD STUFF
DOCUMENT CONTROL

Version: 1.0
Status: IN FORCE
Effective Date: September 12, 2026
Owner: Weird Stuff
Classification: INTERNAL

============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This document defines the mandatory control framework for Weird Stuff documentation.

[MANDATORY]
Controlled documents must have an identifiable title, version, status, owner, classification, and effective date when applicable.

[MANDATORY]
A superseded document must not be treated as the current authoritative version.

============================================================
2. DOCUMENT STATUS
============================================================

IN FORCE
Currently authoritative and applicable.

DRAFT FOR REVIEW
Prepared for review and not yet authoritative.

PENDING
Structure exists but required information or approval remains incomplete.

RETIRED
No longer applicable and retained for historical reference.

ARCHIVED
Superseded or historical material retained under archive controls.

============================================================
3. VERSIONING
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Material changes require a new document version.

[MANDATORY]
The effective date must be updated when a new version enters into force.

[MANDATORY]
Changes to legal documents must not be represented as legally effective until the applicable review and publication requirements have been completed.

============================================================
4. CLASSIFICATION
============================================================

PUBLIC
Approved for public distribution.

INTERNAL
For Weird Stuff personnel and authorized collaborators.

RESTRICTED
For specifically authorized recipients.

CONFIDENTIAL
Information whose unauthorized disclosure could materially harm Weird Stuff, its users, members, or projects.

RESERVED
Information intentionally withheld from publication.

============================================================
5. CHANGE CONTROL
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Important changes must be traceable through version control.

[MANDATORY]
Documents must not be silently replaced while retaining an obsolete version identifier.

[RECOMMENDED]
Material documents should include a revision history.

============================================================
6. ARCHIVE
============================================================

[MANDATORY]
Superseded documents must be retained when historical traceability is required.

[MANDATORY]
Archived documents must not be presented as current policy.

============================================================
END OF DOCUMENT
============================================================
